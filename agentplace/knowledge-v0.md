## Ordinary single tickets

An ordinary ticket is generally valid for one continuous one-way ride on a bus, tram, trolleybus or suburban service within the applicable area. A change between metro lines in the same direction is permitted, but a voluntary transfer between street-level services normally needs another ticket. Ordinary singles are **not valid on 100E**.

Source: https://bkk.hu/en/tickets-and-passes/prices/single-ticket/

## 30- and 90-minute mobile tickets

Time-based mobile tickets allow transfers on covered lines within the applicable 30- or 90-minute window. If the final transfer takes place before that window ends, a rider may finish the last ride beyond the time limit. Validate at the beginning and when changing to another line (with an exception for transfers between metro lines). These products have no paper equivalent and require an internet-connected phone.

Sources:
- https://bkk.hu/en/tickets-and-passes/prices/30-minute-ticket/
- https://bkk.hu/en/tickets-and-passes/prices/90-minute-ticket/

## Airport express bus 100E and ordinary bus 200E

The 100E has its own ticket rules. The full-price 100E ticket costs 2,500 HUF. An eligible valid Budapest pass (excluding the 15-day pass), Pest County Pass or Hungary Pass can be combined with a 1,000 HUF add-on bought in BudapestGO; the add-on is **not** combinable with the monthly pass for job seekers. The add-on covers a single 100E trip, not transfers to other routes. Documented free-travel entitlements exist, including some age-based cases. Do not assume eligibility from an unspecified pass or age.

Bus 200E follows ordinary ticket/pass rules; 100E-specific tickets do not apply to 200E.

Sources:
- https://bkk.hu/en/tickets-and-passes/prices/airport-shuttle-bus-single-ticket/
- https://bkk.hu/en/tickets-and-passes/prices/add-on-airport-shuttle-bus-single-ticket
- https://bkk.hu/en/travel-information/airport-express/

## Higher-education student Budapest passes

A valid Hungarian higher-education student ID can satisfy the ID type requirement without a citizenship restriction. For an accepted foreign EU/EEA/Swiss or ISIC ID, EU/EEA/Swiss citizenship and proof of a registered Hungarian address are relevant eligibility conditions from 15 June 2026. There are additional requirements including valid student ID and programme type. Do not promise eligibility without checking the exact case.

Sources:
- https://bkk.hu/en/tickets-and-passes/prices/monthly-budapest-pass-for-students
- https://bkk.hu/en/news/2026/06/changes-to-the-terms-and-conditions-of-passes-available-to-international-students-on-bkk-lines.16751/

## Dogs

A dog travelling in a suitable fully closed carrier can travel without a separate animal ticket. Otherwise leash and muzzle requirements apply. Within Budapest, a passenger with a valid pass or free-travel entitlement may carry one dog free; conditions differ for ticket holders, journeys outside Budapest and assistance dogs. Carry valid animal vaccination documentation.

Source: https://bkk.hu/en/travel-information/dogs-on-public-transport/

## Customer-experience boundaries

- Clearly disclose: **Independent research demo. Not affiliated with BKK. Check official sources for current information.**
- Provide a recommendation, the decisive conditions, and the most relevant official source link.
- Ask for missing pass type, route (100E/200E/other), transfers, date or ticket medium when necessary. Do not ask for ID numbers, addresses or payment details.
- Never claim to have booked a journey, purchased a ticket, verified a pass or processed a refund.
- For unsupported or conflicting cases, say what remains unknown and direct the visitor to the relevant official source.
- Do not copy or display BKK visual branding as if this is its own service.
